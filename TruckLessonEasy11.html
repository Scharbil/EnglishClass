Reading Section

Officer: Good morning. Please pull onto the scale slowly. I need to check your Gross weight.

Driver: Good morning, Officer. My Dash indicator shows I am under the 80,000-pound Weight limit.

Officer: Your Gross weight is fine at 79,500 lbs, but your trailer Axle is Overweight by 500 lbs.

Driver: I see. I might need to Slide the tandems to balance the load correctly.

Officer: Correct. Go to the parking area, Slide the tandems, and come back to re-weigh.

---------

Officer: Hello. Your Cargo looks heavy. I need to see your ELD / Logbook and your Cargo permit.

Driver: Hello. Here is my Logbook. I am currently On-duty and my Driving limit is not an issue.

Officer: Everything looks good here, but your rear Axle tires look low. Check the Air dryer for leaks.

Driver: Thank you. I will check the pressure on the Shoulder. I don't want a safety Violation.

Officer: Safe travels. Don't forget that being Overweight on a single Axle still results in a Fine.

---------

Officer: You are back. Did you Slide the tandems forward or backward?

Driver: I moved them toward the rear to take weight off the trailer Axle. It was a Slippery surface in the lot.

Officer: Good. Now the weight is distributed properly. Is your Jake brake working for the descent ahead?

Driver: Yes, and I checked my Brake pads while I was Off-duty this morning. Everything is ready.

Officer: Great. Since you fixed the weight, I won't issue a Violation this time. Proceed.

---------

Officer: Stop right there. This Flatbed is carrying an oversize Cargo. Where is your Cargo permit?

Driver: Here it is, Officer. It allows me to exceed the standard Weight limit for this specific Route.

Officer: I see the Cargo permit is valid. However, your Wipers are broken and it's starting to rain.

Driver: I just noticed that. I will pull over to the Shoulder and Tighten the wiper arm immediately.

Officer: Do it now. Driving with poor visibility is Dangerous. I'll give you a Warning, not a Fine.

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, what is the problem with the Axle weight even though the Gross weight is under the limit?
  • What action does the driver take in Conversation A to balance the load, and what is the specific technical term for it?
  • In Conversation B, what mechanical detail does the Officer notice while checking the ELD / Logbook?
  • In Conversation C, how did the driver Slide the tandems to fix the Overweight issue on the trailer?
  • According to Conversation D, why is the driver allowed to exceed the standard Weight limit on that specific Route?
