Reading Section

Q1: How do you identify if there is an issue with your tires?

Concise: I inspect the tread depth and check for uneven wear.

Detailed: I perform a daily walk-around inspection. I look specifically for uneven wear on the tire shoulders, which suggests alignment issues. I also check for any cuts, bulges, or embedded objects that could cause a blowout.

Q2: How do you react if a warning light appears on the dashboard?

Concise: I pull over and check the problem immediately.

Detailed: If a warning light comes on, I don't ignore it. I reduce speed, move to a safe area, and consult the manual or check the operating fluids and electrical systems. I never continue driving if the light indicates a critical failure.

Q3: What do you do when driving through blind spots?

Concise: I check my mirrors frequently and stay alert.

Detailed: I manage blind spots by constantly scanning my mirrors every 5-8 seconds. Before changing lanes, I physically lean forward to get a better angle, ensuring I have full mirror visibility of all surrounding traffic.

Q4: How do you manage your brake system during long descents?

Concise: I use low gears instead of riding the brakes.

Detailed: I manage the air brake system by using engine braking. By selecting a lower gear, I reduce the need to constantly press the pedal, preventing overheating and ensuring the brakes are ready for an emergency response.

Q5: How do surface conditions affect your driving?

Concise: I drive much slower when the road is wet or icy.

Detailed: I constantly monitor the surface conditions. If the road is wet or icy, I increase my following distance by at least three times the normal length and adjust my speed to ensure I have full control of the truck.

Q6: When do you schedule your truck's maintenance?

Concise: I follow the company's maintenance schedule exactly.

Detailed: I strictly adhere to the manufacturer's service interval. Even if the truck feels fine, I ensure that all operating fluids and filters are replaced on time to prevent any unexpected mechanical failure during a long haul.
