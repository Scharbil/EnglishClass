Reading Section

The night shift at the city hospital began with an urgent call regarding a spill in the emergency wing. The cleaning crew had to act quickly to prevent any Pathogen from spreading to other wards. Before entering the area, every technician made sure to put on their full PPE, including double gloves, masks, and protective gowns.

The team leader identified the spill as a Biohazard, requiring a specialized cleanup kit. They followed the hospital's strict Protocol to isolate the zone and prevent any Cross-contamination with the sterile equipment nearby. Every step was executed with precision to ensure that no bacteria could be tracked out of the room on their boots or gear.

During the process, a worker found a discarded needle on the floor that had been missed earlier. He immediately placed it into the yellow Sharps Container to prevent an accidental puncture injury. He then checked the SDS for the industrial-strength cleaner they were using to ensure the correct contact time for total Disinfection of the floor surface.

After the spill was contained, the crew moved to a patient room that required a Terminal Cleaning after a long-term discharge. They applied a specialized Antiseptic solution to high-touch surfaces like bed rails and door handles. The goal was to eliminate any invisible threat, ensuring the environment was perfectly safe for the next patient arrival.
