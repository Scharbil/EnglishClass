Reading Section

Officer: Good morning. Pull over to the Shoulder. Your left Taillights are out.

Driver: Good morning, Officer. I checked the Taillights and the Turn signal at the Headquarters, they were working fine.

Officer: Well, they are not working now. It is Dangerous to drive without a Turn signal in this rain.

Driver: I understand. I will check the Engine fuses. I have a spare bulb in my Cargo.

Officer: Fix it now. I will give you a Warning this time, but don't let it happen again.

---------

Officer: Hello. Is this Flatbed trailer carrying heavy machinery?

Driver: Hello. Yes, the Cargo is an industrial tractor. It is Coupled correctly to the Flatbed.

Officer: I need to check your Safety chain. It looks loose. This is a violation.

Driver: I apologize, Officer. I will Tighten the Safety chain immediately. Here is the Receipt for the security straps.

Officer: The Safety chain must be tight. I have to issue a Fine for this oversight.

---------

Officer: Good afternoon. Stop for a moment. You have Worn tires on the rear axle of your Tanker.

Driver: Good afternoon. I know the tires are old, but I checked the Air pressure this morning.

Officer: The Air pressure is not the only problem. The road has a Slippery surface today. Worn tires are a risk.

Driver: You are right. I will put on my Reflective vest and call the Headquarters for a tire change.

Officer: Good. Stay on the Shoulder until the service truck arrives. Safety is priority.

---------
