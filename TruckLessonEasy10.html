Reading Section

Officer: Good morning. Please show me your ELD / Logbook records for the last seven days.

Driver: Good morning, Officer. Here is my ELD. I have been On-duty for six hours today.

Officer: Your ELD shows you are very close to your Driving limit. When was your last break?

Driver: I took a thirty-minute break on the Shoulder two hours ago. My Dash clock matches the log.

Officer: Correct. Make sure you don't exceed the Driving limit or it will be a serious Violation.

---------

Officer: Hello. You look tired. Did you spend enough time in the Sleeper berth last night?

Driver: Hello. Yes, I was Off-duty for ten hours, and eight of those were in the Sleeper berth.

Officer: I need to verify that. A Violation of rest rules can result in a heavy Fine.

Driver: I understand. I always Tighten my schedule to ensure I am well-rested before hauling Cargo.

Officer: Good. Remember, if you feel sleepy, pull over to the Shoulder immediately.

---------

Officer: Good afternoon. Why is your status Off-duty while you are refueling at the Fuel tank?

Driver: Good afternoon. That was a mistake. I should be On-duty while performing maintenance.

Officer: Exactly. Refueling and checking the Air dryer or Brake pads is considered On-duty time.

Driver: You are right, Officer. I will correct my ELD / Logbook status right now to avoid a Violation.

Officer: Fix it. Falsifying logs is a Dangerous practice that leads to a Fine.

---------

Officer: Good evening. There is Black ice ahead and a snowstorm. Are you stopping soon?

Driver: Good evening. Yes, I am using the adverse driving conditions exception to reach the next Sleeper berth.

Officer: Be careful. Even with an exception, you must not bypass the Driving limit if it is unsafe.

Driver: I agree. My Wipers are at full speed and I am looking for a safe Shoulder or truck stop.

Officer: Use your low beams, not your High beams, and stay safe. Show me your Cargo permit before you go.

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, how many hours has the driver been On-duty today and what is the Officer checking on the ELD?
  • In Conversation B, why is the Sleeper berth record important and what could happen if there is a Violation?
  • According to Conversation C, why is refueling at the Fuel tank considered On-duty time instead of Off-duty?
  • In Conversation D, what exception is the driver using to continue driving despite the Black ice and snow?
  • Based on the dialogues, what is the difference between a Fine and a Violation in a driver's logbook?
