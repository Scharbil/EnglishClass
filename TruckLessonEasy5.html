Reading Section

Officer: Good morning. The Fog is very thick today. Are your Headlights working?

Driver: Good morning, Officer. Yes, my Headlights are on. I am Coming from the Headquarters.

Officer: Be careful. There is a Delay on the Highway due to Traffic. What is your Destination?

Driver: My Destination is the Truck stop. I need to check my Mirrors and my Cargo there.

Officer: Good. Keep your Hazards ready if the Fog gets worse.

---------

Officer: Hello. Why is your Tanker on the Shoulder? Do you have a Breakdown?

Driver: Hello. Yes, I have a mechanical Breakdown. I am waiting for a Tow truck to arrive.

Officer: I see. I need to check your Insurance and your Logbook while we wait.

Driver: Here they are. I have a Receipt for the last maintenance check at the Headquarters.

Officer: The Receipt is good. This is just a safety Inspection. Stay safe on the Shoulder.

---------

Officer: Good afternoon. Please stop for a routine Inspection. I need to see your Manifest.

Driver: Good afternoon. Here is my Manifest. I am carrying Potable water for the city.

Officer: Please check your Mirrors. One is loose. You need to Tighten it.

Driver: You are right, Officer. I will Tighten the Mirrors and check the Wipers before I continue.

Officer: Perfect. Your Logbook is updated. No Warning today. Proceed to your Destination.

---------

Officer: Good evening. Why are you driving so late? Did you have a Delay?

Driver: Yes, I had a Delay at the Weight station. I am Coming from the north reservoir.

Officer: Is your Trailer stable? And do you have enough Fuel for the Route?

Driver: Yes, I stopped at the Truck stop for Fuel. I have the Receipt here.

Officer: Everything is in order. Use your Headlights and follow the Speed limit. Good luck!

  • In Conversation B, why is the driver waiting for a Tow truck on the Shoulder?
  • What did the Officer find loose in Conversation C, and what must the driver do?
  • In Conversation D, why does the driver have a Receipt from the Truck stop?
