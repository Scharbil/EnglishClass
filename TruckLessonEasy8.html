Reading Section

Officer: Good morning. Why are your High beams on? You are blinding other drivers.

Driver: Good morning, Officer. I apologize. A warning light came on my Dash, and I was distracted checking it.

Officer: What does the Dash say? Is it the Coolant level or the engine temperature?

Driver: It indicates a low Coolant level. I need to pull over to the Shoulder and add more liquid.

Officer: Switch off your High beams now. Drive slowly to the next exit and check the Leak.

---------

Officer: Hello. This is a very wide Cargo on your Flatbed. Do you have your Cargo permit?

Driver: Hello. Yes, Officer. I have the Cargo permit right here in my logbook. It is an oversize load.

Officer: I see. Is the load securely Coupled? I also need to verify that your Safety chain is not dragging.

Driver: Everything is secure. I had to Tighten the straps ten miles ago at the Truck stop.

Officer: Good. Remember that without a valid Cargo permit, I would have to issue a very expensive Fine.

---------

Officer: Good afternoon. We are doing a Brake inspection. Your Brake pads look very thin.

Driver: Good afternoon. I was planning to replace the Brake pads at the Headquarters tomorrow.

Officer: That is too late. The road has a Slippery surface today due to the rain. Thin Brake pads won't stop this Tanker.

Driver: I understand the risk. I checked my Mirrors and noticed I am taking longer to slow down.

Officer: It is too Dangerous to continue. You must stay on the Shoulder and call for a mobile mechanic.

---------

Officer: Good evening. Your right Turn signal and Taillights are blinking too fast. There is an electrical fault.

Driver: Good evening, Officer. My Dash did not show any error. I will check the Wipers and the fuses too.

Officer: Also, your Worn tires are a concern. I see almost no tread on the rear ones.

Driver: I am Coming from a long trip. I will change the Worn tires and fix the Turn signal tonight.

Officer: I am giving you a written Warning. If I see this Trailer again in this condition, it will be a Fine.

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, what warning light came on the Dash and what must the driver do with his High beams?
  • In Conversation B, what document is necessary for the wide Cargo on the Flatbed?
  • According to the Officer in Conversation C, why is it dangerous to drive the Tanker with thin Brake pads?
  • In Conversation D, what electrical problem does the Officer notice with the Turn signal and Taillights?
  • Based on Conversation D, what will happen if the Officer sees the Trailer in that condition again?
