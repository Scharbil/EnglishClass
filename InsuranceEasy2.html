Reading Section

Agent: Good morning. I am calling regarding your Risk assessment. Our Underwriter needs updated info for your file.

Client: Good morning. Is this related to my current Policy? I want to avoid any increase in my Premium.

Agent: We just need to review your Driving record to ensure you are eligible for the best rate.

Client: That is fine. I will send you my information so we can complete the Medical questionnaire as well.

Agent: Excellent. This will ensure your Terms and conditions stay accurate for this year.

---------

Agent: Hello. I noticed your payment for this month is late. Did you miss the Grace period?

Client: Hello. I am so sorry. I forgot to choose a new Payment method after my bank changed.

Agent: I understand. Please pay it today to avoid Late fees and a possible Cancellation notice.

Client: I will pay it immediately. Can I divide the annual amount into Installments to make it easier?

Agent: Yes, we can set up Installments for you, but it requires a new authorized agreement.

---------

Agent: Good afternoon. Regarding your recent Claim, our Underwriter has calculated the final Indemnity.

Client: Good afternoon. Does that amount cover the full Market value of the car, considering its Depreciation?

Agent: Yes, we evaluated the car's current condition and applied the standard Depreciation factor.

Client: That seems fair. How long until I receive the payment after signing the Terms and conditions?

Agent: Once you sign, the payment will be processed within three business days.

---------

Agent: Good evening. We offer a new Theft protection package for your home insurance.

Client: Good evening. Does that include any Exclusion for high-value items?

Agent: Not really, but you must list those items in the Application form to be fully covered.

Client: Understood. I want to make sure I am protected against Insurance fraud by having clear documentation.

Agent: Wise choice. I will email the details and an Authorized agent will call you to finalize it.

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, what is the role of the Underwriter in the Risk assessment process, and why does the Agent need the Driving record?
  • In Conversation B, what are the consequences of missing the Grace period for a payment, and what are Installments?
  • According to Conversation C, how does Depreciation affect the final Indemnity payment for a vehicle Claim?
  • In Conversation D, why does the Client want to list high-value items in the Application form despite potential Exclusions?
  • Based on the dialogues, what is the difference between Terms and conditions and a Cancellation notice in the management of a Policy?
