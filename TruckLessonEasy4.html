Reading Section

Officer: Good morning. Please enter the Weight station. I need to verify your Manifest and the total Weight of your Trailer.

Driver: Hello, Officer. My Trailer is carrying Potable water. Here is the Manifest with all the Cargo details.

Officer: Your Weight is correct. Are you Coming from the city plant?

Driver: Yes, I am Coming from the plant and my Destination is the north farm. Is there much Traffic ahead?

Officer: Yes, there is heavy Traffic. Check your Brakes and follow your Route carefully.

---------

Officer: Good afternoon. Why are you parked on the Shoulder of the Highway? Please turn on your Hazards.

Driver: Good afternoon. My Hazards are on now. I stopped because my Wipers are not working in this rain.

Officer: That is Dangerous. I need to see your Insurance and your Logbook for this Inspection.

Driver: Here is my Insurance card, Officer. I also have my Logbook updated. I am Coming from the port.

Officer: Okay. Fix the Wipers before you continue to your Destination. This is a verbal Warning.

---------

Officer: Hello. We are performing a routine Inspection. Is your Tanker leaking any Fuel?

Driver: No, Officer. I checked the Fuel tank and the Brakes this morning. The Cargo is just Potable water.

Officer: Good. Show me your Manifest. Where is your final Destination?

Driver: My Destination is the airport. I took a 45-minute Break at the last station.

Officer: Perfect. Your Logbook looks clean. Be careful with the Speed limit on this Route.

---------

Officer: Good evening. There is a Warning for high winds on the Highway. Is your Trailer stable?

Driver: Yes, Officer. My Trailer is heavy with Potable water, so it is stable. I am Coming from the valley.

Officer: Where are you going? I need to check your Logbook to see your Rest time.

Driver: My Destination is the central station. I had a long Rest yesterday. Here is my Insurance and Registration.

Officer: Very well. Turn on your Hazards if the wind is too strong. Safe Route!
