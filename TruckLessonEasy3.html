Reading Section

Officer: Good morning. Please show me your Driver's license and your vehicle Registration. This is a routine Inspection.

Driver: Good morning, Officer. Here are my documents. I am Coming from the city wells with a Potable water Tanker.

Officer: Your Registration is correct. What is your Destination?

Driver: My Destination is the construction site. I am following the industrial Route.

Officer: I see. I also need to check your Tires and Brakes for safety. Please step out of the truck.

---------

Officer: Hello. You were driving fast. The Speed limit on this Highway is 80 km/h. Is your Cargo very heavy?

Driver: I apologize, Officer. My Tanker is full of Potable water, so the Weight is high. I am Coming from the plant.

Officer: Safety is priority. Your Seatbelt is on, which is good. Show me your Logbook now.

Driver: Yes, sir. I had 11 hours of Rest and a 45-minute Break recently. It is all in the Logbook.

Officer: Fine. Watch your speed and your Brakes. Continue to your Destination.

---------

Officer: Good evening. Why are your Tires so dirty? Have you been on a Dangerous road?

Driver: Yes, Officer. My Route was on a muddy road near the river. I am Coming from the reservoir.

Officer: I need to perform a quick Inspection of the Cargo. Is it Potable water for the hospital?

Driver: Yes, it is. My Destination is the hospital. Here is my Logbook and vehicle Registration.

Officer: Everything looks clean inside. Please drive carefully on the Highway.

---------

Officer: Please pull over. Do you have your Seatbelt on? I need to check your Logbook.

Driver: Yes, Officer. I am always safe. I am Coming from the port with a fresh Cargo.

Officer: Where is your final Destination? And did you have enough Rest?

Driver: I am going to the central station. I had a long Rest of 12 hours and a dinner Break one hour ago.

Officer: Perfect. Your Tires and Brakes look good. Your Route is clear. Good luck!

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, what two documents does the Officer want to see?
  • In Conversation B, what is the Speed limit on the Highway?
  • According to Conversation C, why are the Tires of the Tanker dirty?
  • In Conversation D, what specific mechanical parts did the Officer check?
  • Compare the Rest hours in Conversation B and Conversation D. Are they the same?
