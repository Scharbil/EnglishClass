Reading Section

Dispatcher: Hey, what is your ETA for the delivery? The customer is asking for an update.

Driver: My ETA is 2:00 PM. I had a small Delay at the Scale house because I had to Slide the tandems.

Dispatcher: Copy that. Remember your Driving limit. You don't want an ELD / Logbook violation today.

Driver: Don't worry, I am still On-duty and have plenty of hours. I will arrive before my Appointment.

Dispatcher: Good. Text me when the Load is ready to be empty.

---------

Broker: Hello. I see you are still at the receiver. Is there a Delay?

Driver: Hello. Yes, I have been waiting for three hours. I need to request Detention pay.

Broker: I will check the Rate confirmation. Usually, Detention starts after two hours of waiting.

Driver: Thank you. I am already Off-duty in my Sleeper berth while they unload the Cargo.

Broker: Perfect. Send me the signed BOL later and I will add the Detention to your Rate.

---------

Dispatcher: Why did you stop on the Shoulder? You are losing time for your next Appointment.

Driver: There is Black ice and heavy snow. Safety is my priority to avoid a Fine or an accident.

Dispatcher: I understand, but the Broker is worried about the ETA.

Driver: Tell the Broker there is a weather Delay. I will resume when the Slippery surface is cleared.

Dispatcher: Okay, stay safe. Update your Logbook status to reflect the weather exception.

---------

Broker: The shipper says the Gross weight is higher than expected. Can you still haul the Cargo?

Driver: I am Overweight on my drive Axle. I tried to Slide the tandems but it's not enough.

Broker: If you can't legal the load, we might need a Cargo permit or a different Rate.

Driver: I need a better Rate if I have to take a longer Route to avoid the Scale house.

Broker: Let me talk to the customer. Stay On-duty and wait for my call.

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, what was the reason for the Delay and how did it affect the driver's ETA?
  • In Conversation B, why is the driver requesting Detention pay and what is his current Logbook status?
  • According to Conversation C, why does the driver decide to stop on the Shoulder despite the Dispatcher's concern?
  • In Conversation D, why is the driver unable to "legal the load" by Sliding the tandems?
  • What does the driver negotiate with the Broker in Conversation D to compensate for the Overweight issue and the longer Route?
