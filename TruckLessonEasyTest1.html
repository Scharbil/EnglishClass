Reading Section

— Good morning. Please pull over to the Shoulder for a Level 3 inspection.

— Good morning, Officer. Here is my Driver's license and Registration.

— Thank you. Show me your ELD / Logbook records for today.

— Yes. I am On-duty. I have four hours left in my Driving limit.

— Everything is correct. No Violation found. You can go.

---------

— Hello. Your Gross weight is too high. Please stop at the Scale house.

— Hello. Is my Axle okay? I checked the Cargo this morning.

— No, you are Overweight on the rear. You need to pay a Fine.

— I am sorry. Can I Slide the tandems to fix the weight?

— Yes, fix the weight now and show me your Shipping papers.

---------

— Good afternoon. I need to do a Level 2 inspection of your truck.

— Good afternoon. I will stay Off-duty while you check the vehicle.

— Your Mirrors are good, but your Wipers are very old. This is a Violation.

— I apologize. I have new Wipers in the Sleeper berth. I will change them.

— Good. Also, check your Brake pads. They look thin.

---------

— Stop! The road is dangerous. There is Black ice ahead.

— Thank you, Officer. I am looking for a Shoulder to park safely.

— Good idea. Do not use your Jake brake on this Slippery surface.

— I understand. I will go to my Sleeper berth and wait for the sun.

— Perfect. Stay safe and watch your ELD / Logbook hours.

4. Final Discussion

  • In the first scene, what documents does the Officer ask for?
  • In the second scene, is the truck Overweight? What does the driver need to pay?
  • In the third scene, what part of the truck is a Violation? (Mirrors or Wipers?)
  • In the fourth scene, why is the road dangerous and where does the driver park?
  • According to the text, is it a good idea to use the Jake brake on Black ice?
