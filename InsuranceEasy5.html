Reading Section

Agent: Good morning! I am reaching out because you clicked the Call to action on our recent Facebook Ad campaign.

Client: Oh, yes. I was looking for some information about life insurance online.

Agent: Perfect. When a Lead like you contacts us, we know you are part of our Target audience looking to protect their family.

Client: That is exactly what I am looking for.

---------

Agent: Let me give you a quick Elevator pitch. Our main Hook is that we offer immediate protection from day one.

Client: That sounds good, but what is the Core benefit for me personally?

Agent: Our Value proposition is that we adapt the plan to your specific Customer need, giving you total peace of mind.

Client: I see. That actually sounds very interesting and helpful.

---------

Agent: After reviewing the options, do you have any Objection to the proposed plan?

Client: Well, my Main concern is the monthly cost. I am on a tight Budget right now.

Agent: I completely understand. My goal is to Build trust with you, so we can adjust the Premium to fit your finances without losing benefits.

Client: Thank you. Can we do a Follow-up call tomorrow? I need to check my bank statements.

Agent: Absolutely. I will call you tomorrow morning.

---------

Agent: Good afternoon! Are you ready to Close the deal and secure your Coverage today?

Client: Yes! I am ready to buy this Policy. What is the next step?

Agent: Excellent! The Application process is very fast. I will email you the Agreement right now.

Client: Perfect. Do I need to print it and scan it back to you?

Agent: Not at all. Just add your E-signature from your phone, and we will proceed with the immediate Activation of your insurance.

4. Final Discussion

  • In the first conversation, why is it important to act quickly when a Lead clicks a Call to action from your Ad campaign?
  • In the second dialogue, how does a clear Value proposition and a strong Hook help to quickly address the Customer need?
  • According to the third conversation, what is the best way to handle an Objection regarding a client's Budget to successfully Build trust?
  • In the final conversation, how does using an E-signature speed up the Application process to quickly Close the deal and guarantee Activation?
  • Based on all the dialogues, how does adapting the Premium help the agent secure a signed Agreement for the final Policy?
