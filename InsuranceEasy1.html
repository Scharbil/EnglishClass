Reading Section

Agent: Good morning. I am calling to discuss your Policy. Could you please confirm your current Coverage?

Client: Good morning. I have the basic plan. I have been thinking about increasing my Liability limits today.

Agent: That is a good idea. Your Premium might change, but it provides better protection against Property damage.

Client: I agree. I want to avoid any financial risk if I have an Accident in the future.

Agent: Perfect. I will update your Application form and send the new Quote to your email.

---------

Agent: Hello. I noticed your Life insurance policy is approaching its Expiration date next month.

Client: Hello. Yes, I saw that. Is it possible to handle the Renewal over the phone?

Agent: Yes, of course. I just need to verify your Beneficiary information to ensure the file is complete.

Client: Everything is the same. I just want to ensure my family has full protection without any Exclusion.

Agent: Understood. I will process the Renewal now. You will receive a confirmation letter shortly.

---------

Agent: Good afternoon. Why is the Claim for your vehicle still pending in our system?

Client: Good afternoon. I am sorry. I was waiting for the police report about the Accident to arrive.

Agent: I understand. However, we need that document to calculate your Deductible and process the payment.

Client: You are right. I will send the document right now to avoid any delay with my Claim.

Agent: Great. Once we receive it, we can finalize the settlement for your Personal injury and car repairs.

---------

Agent: Good evening. We have a new Health insurance plan that includes better Coverage for emergencies.

Client: Good evening. That sounds interesting. Does it have a high Deductible like my current one?

Agent: No, this plan is different. It offers lower costs even if you have a medical Claim.

Client: I am interested. Could you send me a Quote so I can compare it with my current plan?

Agent: Absolutely. I will email the details and the Application form so you can review it comfortably.

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, why does the Client want to increase their Liability limits, and how does the Agent explain the change in the Premium?
  • In Conversation B, why is it important for the Agent to verify the Beneficiary information during a Renewal?
  • According to Conversation C, what document does the Agent need to process the Claim and calculate the Deductible?
  • In Conversation D, what is the main benefit of the new Health insurance plan compared to the Client's current one?
  • Based on the dialogues, what is the difference between a Quote and an Application form in the insurance sales process?
