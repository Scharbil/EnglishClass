Reading Section

Agent: Good morning. I understand you are comparing prices. What is the main Objection you have regarding our Premium?

Client: Your Competitive offer is good, but I found a cheaper plan online.

Agent: I understand, but our Value-added services, like 24/7 Assistance, provide more security than standard plans.

Client: That is true. Is there any Loyalty discount if I stay with you for more than a year?

Agent: Absolutely. I can offer you a special rate as part of our Retention program. Shall we proceed with the Closing?

---------

Agent: Hello. I am calling to follow up on your Complaint resolution regarding the recent billing error.

Client: Hello. I am very frustrated. I want to know if I am eligible for a refund according to your Refund policy.

Agent: Yes, we have already processed your refund to ensure your Customer satisfaction.

Client: Thank you. I appreciate the Flexibility you showed in solving this problem quickly.

Agent: You are welcome. We value your trust and want to continue providing the best After-sales service.

---------

Agent: Good afternoon. Would you like to hear about the Benefits of adding Theft protection to your home Policy?

Client: I am not sure. I need to make a Price comparison before I make any decision.

Agent: I can send you a detailed document. Our Sales pitch is simple: full coverage with no hidden costs.

Client: If I sign today, can I get a Referral bonus if I bring a friend to your agency?

Agent: Yes, our Referral program gives you an extra discount on your next renewal.

---------

Agent: Good evening. How is your experience with our After-sales service so far?

Client: Good evening. It has been great. I am impressed by the Complaint resolution process from last month.

Agent: That is our priority. Since you are happy, would you like to explore our Value-added plans for your family?

Client: Send me the information. I like the Flexibility your agency offers compared to others.

Agent: Perfect. I will send the proposal now. I am confident this Competitive offer will meet your needs.

4. Final Discussion

  • In Conversation A, how does the Agent handle the Client's price Objection, and what Value-added services are mentioned?
  • In Conversation B, why is it important for the Agent to prioritize Customer satisfaction after a Complaint resolution?
  • According to Conversation C, what is the main goal of the Referral program, and how does it benefit the Client?
  • In Conversation D, why is After-sales service considered a key factor for the Client's decision to continue with the agency?
  • Based on the dialogues, what is the difference between a Sales pitch and a Competitive offer when trying to close a sale?
